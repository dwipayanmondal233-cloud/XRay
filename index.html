FEATURES

  • Offline OCR. Works with no internet.
  • Tap any word, line or block to copy it.
  • Smart colors for links, emails, phone numbers and promo codes.
  • Open a link, send an email, or dial a number from the highlight.
  • Three triggers: floating bubble, phone shake, Quick Settings tile.
  • Region mode: drag a box to scan only part of the screen.
  • English and Hindi (Devanagari) text.
  • History of your last 20 scans, with copy, share and delete.
  • Share extracted text to any app.

INSTALL

  1. Tap DOWNLOAD APK and open the file.
  2. Allow your browser to install apps if Android asks.
  3. Open XRay and tap OPEN ACCESSIBILITY SETTINGS.
  4. Find XRay and turn it on. XRay uses this only to take a screenshot when you trigger it.
  5. Turn on the floating bubble or shake trigger in the app.
  6. Open any app, trigger XRay, and tap a highlight.

PRIVACY PROMISE

  • Each screenshot is processed in memory only. It is never saved.
  • XRay has no internet permission. It cannot send anything anywhere.
  • No analytics. No accounts. No ads.
  • History saves text only, on your device. Turn it off or clear it any time.

HONEST LIMITS

  • Apps that block screenshots (banking, some video and payment apps) cannot be read. This cannot be bypassed.
  • One scan per second at most. OCR takes about 0.3 to 3 seconds.
  • English and Hindi only. Bengali is not supported.
  • Tiny, stylized, low-contrast or rotated text may be missed or wrong.
  • The overlay is a snapshot. If the screen changes, scan again.
  • This is a debug build shared by link, not from the Play Store.

PROBLEMS AND FIXES

"App blocked to protect your device"

Play Store, profile icon, Play Protect, gear icon, turn off "Scan apps with Play Protect". Install XRay, then turn it back on.

Accessibility switch is greyed out ("Restricted setting")

Settings, Apps, XRay, the 3-dot menu, "Allow restricted settings". Then turn the XRay service on.

After installing an update, nothing works

Turn the XRay accessibility service off and on again.

"App not installed" when updating

Debug builds change signature between versions. Uninstall the old XRay first, then install the new one.

Permission popups do not appear

If any app draws over the screen, Android blocks permission popups. Stop other overlay apps first, or grant permissions manually in Settings.

The bubble or shake stops working

Some phones kill background services. Open Settings, Apps, XRay, Battery, and set it to Unrestricted.

"This app blocks screenshots"

That app uses a secure window. Android does not let any app read it.
